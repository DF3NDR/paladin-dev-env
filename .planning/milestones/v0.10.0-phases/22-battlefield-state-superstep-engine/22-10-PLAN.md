<objective>
Measure the two non-functional claims this epic makes — that per-superstep checkpointing adds under
10 ms p50 on SQLite for a Battlefield at or under 1 MiB, and that the engine holds at most one
Battlefield clone per superstep — and record the measured figures as a baseline.

Purpose: ENG-NFR-02 says "measure, don't guess". A checkpoint-every-superstep design lives or dies
on the marginal cost of the checkpoint, and an unmeasured claim in a released framework is the kind
of thing a user discovers in production.

Output: a criterion bench for the save path, a recorded resident-memory harness, and the measured
numbers written into the summary with their commit.
</objective>
