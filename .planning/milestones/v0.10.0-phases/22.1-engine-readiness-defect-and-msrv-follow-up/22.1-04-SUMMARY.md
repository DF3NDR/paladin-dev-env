- Restored `Cargo.lock` to its pre-`44e13fbd` resolution (`git show 44e13fbd^:Cargo.lock`), reverting 22-17's hand-downgrade of `time`, `process-wrap`, `darling`, `serde_with`, `tonic`, `idna_adapter` and `home`, then re-resolved cleanly under `[workspace] resolver = "3"` with zero lockfile drift beyond the restore itself.

- Retargeted the `msrv` CI job to 1.88 (job name, `RUSTUP_TOOLCHAIN`, `dtolnay/rust-toolchain@1.88`, `Swatinem/rust-cache` key `msrv-1.88`, and the explanatory comment block) and reconciled every MSRV figure in the tree: README's badge (`rust-1.88%2B`) and prerequisites sentence, MIGRATION.md §9.3 (the measured dependency chain, both rejected alternatives, and the X-11.4 default-feature-set confirmation), CHANGELOG.md's new `[Unreleased] ### Changed` entry, and three mdBook pages (`development-setup.md`, `overview.md`, and `stable-api.md`'s two mentions -- the latter two previously claimed an unrelated stale "1.93.1" figure, now corrected to 1.88 alongside everything else).
