    Commit `9802ce60` (`fix(24): CR-02 preserve MusterProgress on a shutdown-grace abort mid-Muster`,
    253 insertions / 6 deletions, `crates/paladin-battalion/src/engine/superstep.rs` only) does three
    things: (1) tracks whether an aborted dispatch-order id came from a Muster task
    (`muster_task_aborted: bool`) separately from ordinary aborted vanguard nodes
    (`aborted_node_ids`); (2) in the Halted-Waypoint branch, stops re-adding a Muster worker's
    `NodeId` to `halted_vanguard` (it is recovered via `MusterProgress::unfinished_tasks()` instead)
    and preserves the round's in-flight `MusterProgress` via `aborted_muster_progress` instead of
    hard-coding `None`; and (3) gates the pre-existing "fold this Muster's completed-task deltas into
    `deltas`" step on `!muster_task_aborted`, deferring that fold to the resumed round exactly once.

    Change (3) was not in the review's illustrative snippet. Without it, the fix as originally
    sketched double-merged every already-completed sibling task's delta on resume — caught by the new
    regression test failing with `["a","a","b","b","c","d","d","e","e"]` instead of
    `["a","b","c","d","e"]`.

    Automated evidence already exists and was independently re-run during verification:
    `shutdown_grace_abort_mid_muster_preserves_progress_for_resume` passes, as do the full
    564-test unit suite and all six named integration binaries.
