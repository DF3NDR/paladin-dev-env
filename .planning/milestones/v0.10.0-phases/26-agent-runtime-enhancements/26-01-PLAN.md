<objective>
Land the `ExecutionMiddleware` seam end-to-end: the trait and its two flow enums, the per-run
`ModelCallContext` / `ToolCallContext` / `LlmResponseView` / `FinalResult` value types, the
structured `PromptAssembly` that replaces the flat prompt buffer, the onion-ordering chain driver,
and the four hook call sites inside `PaladinExecutionService`'s reasoning loop — proven by one
recording middleware observing the full `before → model → after → tool` sequence on a real run, and
by a golden test showing an **empty** chain reproduces today's bytes exactly.

This is the phase's **tracer**. It is production code, not a prototype: the trait lands in its final
shape (nothing after this plan reshapes `ExecutionMiddleware`, `MiddlewareFlow`, `ToolFlow` or the
contexts), every one of the eleven built-ins in plans 26-05 … 26-19 is written against these exact
types, and the single wired path carries a real end-to-end `<verify>`. Everything after this plan is
horizontal expansion from the proven slice rather than an architectural bet discovered ten committed
layers later.

Purpose: prove the one architectural risk of RT-01 — that a hook chain can be threaded through a
reasoning loop whose prompt is a flat string, without changing a byte of that string when the chain
is empty (D-02's locked invariant, X-03).

Output: `middleware/{mod,chain,context}.rs`; assembly-based prompt construction and four hook sites
in `paladin_execution_service.rs`; the two-layer rustdoc on `NodeInterceptor` and
`ExecutionMiddleware`; `tests/integration/middleware_under_engine_test.rs`.
</objective>
