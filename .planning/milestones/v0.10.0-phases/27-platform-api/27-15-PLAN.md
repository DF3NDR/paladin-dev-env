|----------|-------------|
| Internet → every new `/v1` route | Untrusted principals, query strings (`limit`, `cursor`, filters) and bodies. |

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-27-15-01 | Elevation of privilege | mutating routes without proper scope | high | mitigate | `require_admin` on assistant/schedule/thread-delete; `authorize_invoke`/`allowed_roles` on submit/cancel/fork; `run_controller_auth` tests. |
| T-27-15-02 | Denial of service | unbounded pagination / cursor abuse | high | mitigate | `resolve_limit` 1..=100 typed 400; `decode_cursor` typed 400 with no scan; keyset queries hit indexes. |
