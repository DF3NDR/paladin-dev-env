<objective>
Establish the authoritative trace model end to end: the twelve-variant `TraceEvent` and its
`TraceRecord` envelope in `paladin-core`, the `TraceEmitter` handle and `CompositeSink` fan-out in
`paladin-ports`, and a `TraceDispatcher` that stamps `seq`/`at`/`thread_id` at enqueue time, isolates
sink panics, and accounts for every drop. Every existing producer and consumer in the workspace is
migrated to the envelope in the same change so the tree stays green.

Purpose: OBS-01 / OBS-FR-01…03. This is the contract every other plan in Phase 28 consumes — sinks,
OTel spans, SSE mapping, persistence, the inspector and the eval harness all read `TraceRecord`.
Decomposition follows D-41's wave 1.

Output: `crates/paladin-core/src/platform/container/trace.rs`, an extended `trace_sink_port.rs`, a
stamping/panic-isolating `TraceDispatcher`, a `PanickingTraceSink` test double, the D-06 ordering and
overflow tests, and the X-05 multi-thread stress test.
</objective>
