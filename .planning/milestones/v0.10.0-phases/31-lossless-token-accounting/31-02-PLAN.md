<objective>
Break every carrier above the LLM port over to a full `TokenUsage` in one coordinated change:
`PaladinResult.usage`, `NodeExecutionRecord.usage`, `TraceEvent::NodeFinished.usage`,
`TraceEvent::RunFinished.usage`, real per-Paladin splits in `BattalionResult.per_paladin_tokens`,
and the deletion of the total-only constructor that caused the zeroing bug (ACCT-02; D-05, D-07,
D-08, D-09, D-10, D-11, D-12, D-25, D-30).

Purpose: this is the keystone change of the phase. Until `PaladinResult` carries the split, the
`TokenUsage` work in plan 31-01 has nowhere to go, and every layer above re-collapses it. Because
the workspace cannot compile in a half-migrated state, this lands as one plan; the compiler
enumerates every site, so the work is mechanical rather than exploratory.

Output: a workspace that compiles and tests green with a lossless carrier chain from
`PaladinResult` to `RunFinished`, proven by a round-trip test over two nodes with distinct
non-round figures.
</objective>

<assumption_delta_decision>
**Noun now primary:** `TokenUsage` (the full prompt/completion/cache/reasoning carrier).

**Decision: `promote`.** The bare token total loses its monopoly as *the* token carrier.
`TokenUsage` is promoted to the primary carrier on `PaladinResult`, `NodeExecutionRecord`,
`TraceEvent::NodeFinished` and `TraceEvent::RunFinished`; the bare count is demoted to a derived
convenience that may only coexist *beside* a full carrier, never stand alone (D-07, D-08).
`BattalionResult.total_tokens: u64` legitimately stays because its sibling `per_paladin_tokens`
already carries the full split at the same level — that is coexistence, not a surviving monopoly.

**Not `add-alongside`.** Keeping the bare count next to a new `usage` on the four promoted carriers
would leave two sources of truth that can disagree, and would ship exactly the compatibility shim
ADR-0051 supersedes X-03 to avoid.

**Companion invariant test (accepted):** the D-30 round-trip test in task 1 is the invariant test
for the generalized intent — it asserts field-by-field equality of the *full* carrier across
`NodeExecutionRecord`, `NodeFinished` and the summed `RunFinished`, so a future regression that
re-collapses any hop to a total fails rather than passing on a matching total.
</assumption_delta_decision>
