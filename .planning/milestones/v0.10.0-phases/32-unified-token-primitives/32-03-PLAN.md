<objective>
Retire the duplicate counting contract. The legacy fallible trait and its factory are deleted
outright with no deprecated retention (D-09) — the research grep confirms zero in-tree callers
outside the definition file and the three re-export sites — and the tiktoken adapter keeps exactly
the surface D-10 names: fallible construction, an inherent model-name accessor, the cache
accessors, and its impl of the counting port with the BPE lookup and per-string cache inlined
straight into `count`. The four re-export sites narrow to the surviving type inside their unchanged
feature gates (D-11), and every doc site that named the removed pair is swept (D-12).

Purpose: with the exactness signal now on the port (plan 32-01), the legacy trait is not merely
redundant — it is a second counting contract that a future adapter could implement instead of the
real one, quietly reintroducing the split this phase exists to close. Deleting it is what makes
"exactly one counting contract" a fact rather than a convention.

The one-way rating on this removal (D-09) was gated once, together with the constructor break, by
the consolidated checkpoint in plan 32-01; this plan carries the rating for the record and opens no
second stop. The no-shim prohibition recorded in that plan governs this removal too.

Output: a 160-line-lighter `token_counter.rs` whose only counting path is the port; four narrowed
re-export lines with their feature gates intact; four swept doc sites; and an exit grep over the
tracked source tree that finds neither removed name.
</objective>
