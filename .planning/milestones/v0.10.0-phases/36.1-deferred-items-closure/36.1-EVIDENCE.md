## Spec-less probe note

Phase 36.1 has no `SPEC.md`. The deterministic edge-coverage probe the phase's own tooling would
normally run against a specification document was skipped at planning time because the phase had
no requirement identifiers to probe against -- `CURR-16` through `CURR-21` were minted during that
same planning run, after the point at which a spec-less probe would have needed them to exist.
Consequently the `must_haves.prohibitions` recorded in each plan's frontmatter (including this
plan's own seven prohibitions) were authored by recall against the phase's decisions, not verified
against an automated edge-coverage tool, and they carry no automated check path beyond the plan's
own `<verify>` blocks and acceptance criteria. This is stated plainly here rather than implied by
omission.
