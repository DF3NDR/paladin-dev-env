## Accomplishments

- Reproduced the real `v0.10.0` `create-release` failure mode as a committed regression case: `tests/scripts/create-or-reuse-release_test.sh` case 9 feeds a stubbed 200000-character lookup body through the real, unmodified script and observed the exact signal-driven abort — exit **141** (SIGPIPE, `pipefail`) — with 20 of 21 assertions still passing in the same run (only the new case failed), confirming the red is isolated and the failure is the expected class, not a wrong-output mismatch.
- Fixed the defect at its source rather than tuning around it: `_cor_gh_call`'s status-line extraction now uses bash parameter expansion (`${raw%%$'\n'*}` then `${status_line//$'\r'/}`) with no subprocess and no pipe — the entire EPIPE failure class has nothing left to act on. The status-code and body-extraction lines immediately below are byte-for-byte unchanged.
- Confirmed behavioural equivalence on both named edge cases rather than assuming it: an empty captured response and a single-line response with no trailing newline both produce identical output between the old `head -n1` form and the new parameter-expansion form, exercised side-by-side in the same shell session and recorded verbatim.
- Turned the regression case green: `./tests/scripts/create-or-reuse-release_test.sh` now passes all 21 assertions (was 20/21 before the fix), `make lint-shell` reports shellcheck clean, and `make test-shell-guards` runs all ten guard suites green with no working-tree mutation.
- Re-swept the release/guard scripts against the current tree (not transcribed from RESEARCH.md) and recorded three further sites sharing the same early-closing-reader-under-`pipefail` shape — `check-deprecations.sh:32`, `check-doc-examples.sh:142` and `:164`, `check-public-api-examples.sh:136` — plus the sites confirmed structurally safe (here-string inputs). Stated both halves of the verdict explicitly: the narrow literal-form claim is confirmed, the broader implication that no other instance of the race class exists is overturned. None of the three sites was touched — assigned to CONTEXT.md's deferred wider release-tooling audit per D-00d.

## Decisions Made

- **Parameter expansion over a here-string or an EOF-draining `sed`:** RESEARCH.md verified all three candidate forms fix the observed 200KB stub scenario, but only parameter expansion removes the subprocess-and-pipe machinery the whole EPIPE class depends on — the other two forms avoid triggering the race in testing without eliminating the mechanism that causes it. Chosen per CONTEXT.md's discretion menu and PATTERNS.md's quoted exact replacement.
- **Regression payload sized at 200000 characters via a named variable, not a magic literal near the textbook buffer size:** RESEARCH.md measured ~70KB as a reliable non-trigger and 100KB+ as a reliable trigger (5/5) on this hardware; a marginally-sized payload would produce a test that sometimes passes against broken code, which is worse than no test. `PADDING_SIZE=200000` is set once and reused, not pasted inline as a bare literal in the fixture-generation call.
- **The three swept sites are recorded, not fixed:** per D-00d and this plan's own prohibition, fixing `check-deprecations.sh`, `check-doc-examples.sh`, or `check-public-api-examples.sh` would widen this release branch's diff outside SC2's stated intent (one function, one assignment, in one file). All three are assigned to CONTEXT.md's named deferred item, "A wider first-run audit of the release scripts."
