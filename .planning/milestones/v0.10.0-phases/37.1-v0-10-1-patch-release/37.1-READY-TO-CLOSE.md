## Success Criteria — restated against what was observed

### SC1 — `paladin-battalion` packages and publishes at its `CRATES` position, no forward-pointing versioned dev-dependency

**Observed: TRUE.** `paladin-battalion`'s two versioned workspace dev-dependencies
(`paladin-llm`, `paladin-storage`) were made path-only, so Cargo strips the `version` field from
the published manifest and neither imposes a registry-ordering constraint. `CRATES` order was
left unchanged. Evidence: `37.1-CI-EVIDENCE.md` § "Local sweep" (the SC3 gate proven red on the
`1d4a9724` tree, then green after this fix) and plan `37.1-04`'s own commit
(`crates/paladin-battalion/Cargo.toml`).

### SC2 — `scripts/create-or-reuse-release.sh` no longer fails on response size

**Observed: TRUE.** The `printf … | head -n1` under `set -o pipefail` at line 79 (an `EPIPE` race
that lost repeatedly against `v0.10.0`'s 46 KB release response) was replaced with a form that has
no early-closing reader, with a regression test feeding a response larger than the pipe buffer.
Evidence: `37.1-CI-EVIDENCE.md` § "Local sweep" and plan `37.1-05`'s commit
(`scripts/create-or-reuse-release.sh`, `tests/scripts/create-or-reuse-release_test.sh`).

### SC3 — A gate exercises the real per-crate resolution path, not only `cargo publish --workspace --dry-run`

**Observed: TRUE.** A static ordering gate asserts, for every `CRATES` position, that every
versioned workspace dependency (normal, build **and dev**) of that crate appears earlier in the
array — crate set and edges derived from `cargo metadata`, the array read through
`scripts/publish-crates.sh`'s `PUBLISH_CRATES_LIB_ONLY=1` sourcing seam. It runs in
`make publish-dry-run`/`release-check` and in CI (wired into the `License & Dependency Policy`
job, not `publish-dry-run` — see the findings list below for why). Proven **red** on a captured
copy of the `1d4a9724` tree naming `paladin-battalion` → `paladin-llm`/`paladin-storage`, then
green on the fixed tree. Evidence: `37.1-CI-EVIDENCE.md` § "Local sweep", plans `37.1-02`/`37.1-03`.

### SC4 — Twelve manifests at `0.10.1`, gate set re-sealed, PR merges with a merge commit, sign-off before merge, annotated tag on the merge commit after green `ci.yml`

**Observed: TRUE, and the process-order defect Phase 37 recorded is fixed structurally, not just
by intent.** All twelve publishable manifests plus the non-publishable `doc-examples` manifest,
their changelogs, and the `MIGRATION.md`/allowlist rows read `0.10.1`. The Phase 29 gate set was
re-sealed twice (corpus audit §13, then §14 after the API-surface nightly pin) on the final
commit. **The maintainer's sign-off tick commit (`f3fc061d`) exists and precedes the merge
commit** — `tree(merge) == tree(tick)`, and the merge commit (`f7dae267`) has two parents,
`1d4a9724` (previous `main` tip) and `f3fc061d` (the tick) — a true merge, not squash or rebase.
This is the exact ordering Phase 37 recorded as a process-order deviation (merge happened before
the tick); Phase 37.1's D-10 made it structurally enforced instead of merely stated, and it held.
The annotated `v0.10.1` tag sits on that merge commit, pushed after `ci.yml` concluded `success`
on it. Evidence: `37.1-CI-EVIDENCE.md` § "Plan 37.1-12 Task 1" (tag-equality proof) and the
`f3fc061d` tick-commit record above it.

### SC5 — `release.yml` runs green, every publishable crate resolves at `0.10.1` (`yanked = false`), `paladin-eval` shows non-null `trustpub_data`

**Observed: TRUE.** Release run `35659477719`, attempt 3: `success` (attempts 1-2 explained —
a Trusted Publisher environment-name mismatch on `paladin-eval`, corrected by the maintainer).
All 12 `cargo metadata`-derived publishable crates read `0.10.1` present, checksum recorded,

commit), contrasted against its earlier token-published `0.0.1` bootstrap's `null` reading; two
further crates sampled show the identical non-null shape. Evidence: `37.1-CI-EVIDENCE.md` §
"Registry verification" and § "Trusted-publishing proof" (plan `37.1-13`).

### SC6 — The three orphaned `0.10.0` versions explicitly dispositioned; MILESTONES.md, the `v0.10.0` GitHub Release, and Phase 37's open plans tell the true story

**Observed: TRUE.** The maintainer yanked all three orphans (`paladin-ai-core`, `paladin-ports`,
`paladin-herald`, all `0.10.0`) with their own credential, only after the registry read every
