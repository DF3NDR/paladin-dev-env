<objective>
Give the engine's trace stream the cost carriers and the run-level aggregation (D-10, D-11a):
`NodeFinished.cost`, `RunFinished.cost`, and `TraceDispatcher::total_cost()` as the synchronous
twin of `total_usage()`, with every `RunFinished` emission site populated from it.

Purpose: `RunFinished` is the engine run's audit record and the input the engine-side
`ExecutionMetadata` producer (38-08) reads. Plan 38-07 then fills `NodeFinished.cost` from each
Paladin attempt's `PaladinResult.cost`; this plan lands the carriers, the migration and the
aggregation rule so 38-07 is a one-site bridge. File ownership forces the split: `PaladinResult`'s
literal sites overlap files other wave-3 plans own.

Output: two new enum-variant fields with legacy-compatible serde, ~63 migrated literals/patterns
across ~18 files, `total_cost()`, and five populated emission sites.

**Tracer:** expansion of plan 38-02's tracer.
**Reversibility:** D-10 is rated costly (public trace-event shape). Flagged, not gated.
</objective>
