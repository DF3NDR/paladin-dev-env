<objective>
Put the agent-loop cutoff on the HTTP agent routes: every per-agent service carries the one
`TokenBudget`, the routes admit with the agent's model and hand the derived budget to the run, a
crossed budget answers `allowance_halted` with its `halt_reason`, and unpriced models are refused.

Split from the original 42-07 at plan-check revision 1 (scope_sanity): 42-07 built the derivation and
the Treasurer-aware cutoff; this plan installs and wires them on the HTTP surface; 42-09 does the
worker's agent-kind path.

Task 1 installs the cutoff on every per-agent service and prices the boot-time Treasurer. Task 2
wires the routes (admission with the model, the derived budget on every route's `RunScope`, the wire
label and DTO field, the 422, the golden exception) and proves the halt end to end, including the
buffered stream's `done` that carries D-12's stream clause. Task 3 registers and documents it.

Purpose: ALLOW-05 and ALLOW-03's agent-loop halt on the HTTP routes (D-09..D-13), with D-12's stream
clause made explicit rather than silently narrowed.

Output: the per-agent cutoff, `stop_reason: "allowance_halted"` with `halt_reason`, the
`422 model_unpriced` refusal, the v0.9 golden exception, registers and docs.
</objective>
