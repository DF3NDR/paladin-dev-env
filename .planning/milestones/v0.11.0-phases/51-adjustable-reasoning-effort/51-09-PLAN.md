<objective>

response metadata, and make the node cache key effort-aware without disturbing any existing key.

answer, research Pitfall 7), REASON-07 (the `TraceEvent` field break registered). Research Open Question 5: CONTEXT

or exhaustively destructuring sites in 17 files, measured).

the registered break, CHANGELOG entries and a refreshed baseline.
