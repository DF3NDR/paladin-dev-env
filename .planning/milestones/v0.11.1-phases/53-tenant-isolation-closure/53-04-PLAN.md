Admin keeps the current single-fetch path.

<planner_decisions>
- **PD-04-1 (orchestrator note 6, research A2):** `MAX_EXAMINED_THREADS: usize = 500` and `THREAD_SCAN_BATCH: u32 = 100`, both private module constants. The cap bounds the per-request cost to at most five list calls and 500 owner lookups.
- **PD-04-2 (AC-02 reading):** `{ "items": [], "next_cursor": null }` is the answer when the walk exhausts the store with nothing visible. When the cap stops the walk first, the page may be empty with a non-null cursor; the client continues and the walk ends with `next_cursor: null`. Both shapes are tested.
- **PD-04-3 (orchestrator note 2):** inside the walk, a `Serialization` from the owner lookup is "not visible" for a `Tenant` scope (log and skip, so one tenant's corrupt row cannot fail another tenant's list -- research Pitfall 8); other run-repository errors are the fixed 500 for every role.
- **PD-04-4:** the exclusive-timestamp cursor can skip two threads with an identical `last_updated_at` straddling a boundary; this is a pre-existing property of `WaypointPort::list_threads` (the route already documents a keyset walk, not a snapshot) and is restated in the rustdoc, not changed.
</planner_decisions>
