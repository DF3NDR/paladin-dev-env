## Why the previously-cited evidence did not meet the bar

Checkpoint 22-17 closed G-22-1 on a job (`Postgres Waypoint Contract Suite (live
server)`, `success`, id `100440861780`) inside run **`33688238662`** (commit
`2b2bc1d5`). That job was green, but the **run's own overall conclusion was
`cancelled`** — superseded by a later push under `ci.yml`'s `concurrency` group
(line 26) — and three required jobs inside that same run had already failed
before the supersession: `MSRV (Rust 1.85)`, `Security Audit`, and `License &
Dependency Policy`. A green job inside a run whose overall verdict is not
`success` is not evidence that the workflow, as a whole, passed on that commit;
D-12 restates the bar as a whole-run `success` for exactly this reason.

## What this evidence proves, and what it does not

**Proves:** the `waypoint::postgres` contract suite (26 of 26 declared tests)
executed against a live Postgres server, inside a `ci.yml` run whose *overall*
conclusion was `success`, on a commit containing every other Phase 22.1 change
(the readiness fix and its typed guards, the fingerprint completeness fix, and
the MSRV floor raise to 1.88 that this run's own green `MSRV (Rust 1.88)` job
confirms). The three jobs that failed in the previously-cited run — MSRV,
Security Audit, License & Dependency Policy — are all `success` here, on the
same commit.

**Does not prove:** anything about runs on any other commit, or about this
commit's behavior under conditions this suite does not exercise (e.g.
concurrent writers, network partition between the runner and the container).
G-22-1's truth is scoped to "passes the shared Tier 2 contract suite against a
real Postgres server, identically to SQLite/InMemory" — this evidence is scoped
identically.
