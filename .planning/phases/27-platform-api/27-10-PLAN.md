<objective>
Bridge live run progress to SSE with a documented degraded mode: a per-run broadcast bus fed by a
`TraceSink` adapter (live-progress events) and by the worker (terminal and parley events), consumed
by `paladin-web` through an input port that returns a stream of core events.

Purpose: PLAT-FR-07. Research proved today's `TraceEvent` cannot produce `parley`/`error`, so the bus
has two producers by design (D-25 correction); Phase 28 later collapses the mapping into one place
when the authoritative enum lands.

Output: `RunStreamEvent`/`RunStreamEventKind`/`RunStreamMode` (core), `RunEventStreamPort` (ports),
`events.rs` (facade bus, sink, live/degraded service), worker publishing, the SSE route with 15 s
keep-alive, `stream_tests.rs`, regenerated `openapi.json`.
</objective>
