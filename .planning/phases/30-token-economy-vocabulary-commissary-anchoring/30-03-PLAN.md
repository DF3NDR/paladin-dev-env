rather than live, and retire the last two prose references to the superseded budgeting term.

the compiled tree without touching the planning history that legitimately records the port.

and one historical annotation. **No behaviour, signature, field or config key changes.**

phase, per `references/planner-mvp-mode.md`). This plan is expansion.

introduces the reserved spend-governance officer's name into `crates/` as documentation prose,
