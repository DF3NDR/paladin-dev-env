<objective>
Give the streaming path a usage contract and implement it for the OpenAI-compatible family: a
terminal-chunk rule on the port (`StreamingResponse.usage`, `ChunkMetadata.usage`), the
`stream_options` request field and usage-frame parsing in `CompatEngine`, `openai` and `deepseek`,
and the no-estimation fallback in the execution service (ACCT-03; D-13, D-14, D-15, D-17, D-18,
D-20).

Purpose: today a streamed run reports no usage at all, so the Treasurer could never price a
streamed call the way it prices a non-streamed one. The contract must be ONE rule across every
adapter — `usage` is `Some` on exactly the chunk that carries `finish_reason` — or the shared
conformance case in plan 31-04 cannot exist.

Output: the port contract, its constructors, and the three OpenAI-family streaming
implementations, with the execution service carrying the figure out to its streaming consumer.

## Prohibition recall (spec-less fallback)

Two prohibitions are authored into `must_haves.prohibitions` above (fabricated `Some(0)`; an
estimate substituted for a billed count). One further candidate — "a raw provider response body
must not reach a log or error string unredacted from the new usage-frame parsing code" — is canon
security (OWASP information-disclosure) and is therefore referred to the project's existing control
rather than minted as a prohibition: it is covered by `.github/instructions/security.instructions.md`'s
credential-handling review and by threat `T-31-08` below.
</objective>
