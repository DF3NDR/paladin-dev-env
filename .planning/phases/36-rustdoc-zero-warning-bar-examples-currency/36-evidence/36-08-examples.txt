changes permitted): `PaladinError::ArmamentFailed`'s own doc comment

line 1648 and the handoff arm around line 1769) build `reason: e.to_string()` directly, never
routing through `ToolResultFormatter::sanitize_tool_text` (the module's single `redact_secret_patterns`
+ `bounded_excerpt` sanitization point). Reproduced live above: the fail-run reason field prints the
fake credential (`Bearer sk-live-demo0123456789`) verbatim, unredacted. Recorded in this plan's
SUMMARY.md deviations and appended to `.planning/WINDOWS.md`.
