+ `bounded_excerpt` sanitization point). Reproduced live above: the fail-run reason field prints the
fake credential (`Bearer sk-live-demo0123456789`) verbatim, unredacted. Recorded in this plan's
SUMMARY.md deviations and appended to `.planning/WINDOWS.md`.
