## Phase Boundary

Every deferred item Phases 30-35 recorded and left unowned is closed in the tree or explicitly
dispositioned before v0.10.0 ships. The inputs are fixed: the four per-phase registers
(`31`, `32`, `34`, `35` `deferred-items.md`) plus the Phase 36 register, which was opened after
the roadmap entry was written and names this phase as its recommended owner; `WINDOWS.md` rows
36-37 (plus the Phase 36 row 38, likewise filed after the roadmap entry); and the two
`todos/pending/` files. Each
item is **fixed**, **waived with a written reason**, or **re-homed to a named owner with a
re-check date**, and the ledger is brought back into agreement with the registers so
`/gsd-complete-milestone v0.10.0` sees the whole picture.

Delivered here: the `docs/src` prose fixes SC1 names; the `# Examples`-gate disposition SC2
names (wire + refreeze + fix the 19); the `cli_isolation` rescoping (SC3); the ledger
reconciliation (SC4); the two todo dispositions (SC5); a bounded fix for ledger row 38; the
closing gates green (SC6); a written, re-homed disposition for the eight crate-level rustdoc
`allow` attributes the Phase 36 register hands to this phase (kept and justified, not fixed).
**Not** delivered here: any new capability, any new `docs/src` page,
any rewrite beyond the cited lines, the RustFS adapter itself, the Docker coverage walk itself,
or any change to a public API item (the api-surface baseline must not move).
