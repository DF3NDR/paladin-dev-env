<objective>
Carry cost on the non-streaming LLM response and price it (D-10, D-09): add `LlmResponse.cost`,
migrate every in-tree struct literal in the same commit, register the break per X-03, and make
`PricingLlmAdapter::generate` price each response from its own served model — including across a
`FallbackLlmAdapter` hop.

Purpose: the engine path (`execute_scoped` → `generate`) and the agent loop's buffered path both
consume `LlmResponse`; until it carries a `Cost`, neither can report spend. 38-02 priced only
streams.

Output: the additive field, ~76 migrated literals across ~37 files, `generate` pricing with a
fallback-hop test, and the MIGRATION/allowlist/lint registration.

**Why a MIGRATION row despite D-00g's "no row expected":** `LlmResponse` is constructible, not
`#[non_exhaustive]`, has no `Default`, and existed at the `v0.9.0` baseline CI's `semver` job diffs
against, so adding a public field fires `constructible_struct_adds_field` for `paladin-ports` (a
lint that crate does not yet allow). D-00g's binding rule — any break needs a §9.2 row and an
allowlist entry — therefore applies; its "no row expected" was a design expectation, and the
shipped tree outranks it (D-00i). `PaladinResult`/`Settings`/`TraceEvent` registrations are
measured empirically in 38-09.

**Tracer:** expansion of plan 38-02's tracer (one tracer per phase).
**Reversibility:** D-10 is rated costly (four public structs gain a field; removal is an X-03
break). Flagged, not gated.
</objective>
