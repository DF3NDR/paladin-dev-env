<objective>
Carry cost on `PaladinResult` and close the two remaining gaps in the cost chain (D-10): the agent
loop sums its model calls' costs into `PaladinResult.cost`, and the engine turns each Paladin
attempt's `PaladinResult.cost` into `NodeFinished.cost`, so 38-06's `TraceDispatcher::total_cost()`
yields a real `RunFinished.cost` end-to-end.

Purpose: after 38-04 every `LlmResponse` is priced; after this plan both run paths aggregate those
per-call costs into their run-level carriers, which is what the engine-side `ExecutionMetadata`
producer (38-08) and every trace consumer read.

Output: the additive `PaladinResult.cost` field with its literal migration, the agent-loop
`CostTally`, the engine `NodeDispatchResult` bridge, and end-to-end tests on both paths.

**Tracer:** expansion of plan 38-02's tracer.
**Reversibility:** D-10 rated costly — flagged, not gated. The `PaladinResult` break is already
suppressed crate-wide (`constructible_struct_adds_field = "allow"` in `crates/paladin-core/Cargo.toml`)
and its §9.2 row exists; 38-09 extends that row's Change cell after measuring.
</objective>
