<objective>
Make draws race-proof and settlement idempotent, and pin both with the shared contract suite
(LEDGR-01/02/03).

Task 1 writes the phase's first red test — the LEDGR-02 N−1-of-N race clause — together with the
reserve/release/balance-math clauses, grows `TreasuryLedgerPort` by `reserve` and `release`, and
implements them (plus the reserved-settle path 39-01 left to this plan) on SQLite under
`BEGIN IMMEDIATE`. Task 2 completes the suite (idempotency, spend grouping/ordering/windows,
validation, store clock) and adds `InMemoryTreasuryLedger`, wiring every clause into both adapters.

Purpose: the race and the duplicate-settle guarantees are the phase's central correctness
properties; they must hold by construction (store-side serialization and a unique index), be proven
under true multi-connection concurrency, and be proven by the same functions on every adapter.

Output: `contract_tests.rs`, `in_memory.rs`, `reserve`/`release` on the port and on SQLite.
</objective>
