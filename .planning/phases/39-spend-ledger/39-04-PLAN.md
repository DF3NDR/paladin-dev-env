ADR-0052 names, aggregating every priced Paladin attempt of that superstep (LEDGR-03 key shape,

attached exactly where Phase 42 will later reserve and halt; D-07 makes the key's `attempt` the
persisted `Run.attempt`, handed in by the worker (39-07) through `SettlementContext`.

the superstep loop (including nested Battalion runs), and engine tests using `InMemoryTreasuryLedger`
(a dev-dependency only).
</objective>
