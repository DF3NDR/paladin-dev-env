<objective>
Deliver ALLOW-04's webhook leg: an operator-level allowance notice through the existing durable
webhook machinery (D-17), with C3 Option A's event discriminator and correlation row, an operator
signing secret that never touches the row, the SSRF guard at boot and send time, and the rollout
guidance for mixed-version replicas.

Purpose: the operator learns a tenant or key is about to exhaust its allowance before callers start
receiving 429s.

Output: `RunEventKind::AllowanceWarning` + `as_str`, `AllowanceWarningPayload`, the delivery
service's operator branch, `Treasurer::with_operator_webhook`, boot-time SSRF check, registers and docs.
</objective>
