<objective>
Give the agent loop its allowance cutoff as a proven library capability: the Treasurer derives a
per-run token budget from the remaining allowance and the model's dearest price, and the one existing
`TokenBudget` middleware enforces the tighter of that figure and the operator's own -- composed with,
never replacing, `ModelCallLimit`, `ToolCallLimit` and the Commissary.

Split from the original 42-07 at plan-check revision 1 (scope_sanity): this plan carries the
derivation and the cutoff; 42-08 installs the cutoff on every per-agent service and wires the HTTP
agent routes; 42-09 the worker's agent-kind path.

Task 1 lands the value types (`StopReason::AllowanceHalted`, `DerivedTokenBudget`, the `RunScope`
carrier, the `Admission` field), the defaulted `admit_for_model` port method and the unpriced-model
refusal, and the Treasurer's derivation over the shared evaluation. Task 2 makes `TokenBudget`
Treasurer-aware (tightest wins), plumbs the figure from `RunScope` to the per-call context, and
proves the composition with the call limits and the Commissary on a real service. Task 3 registers
the new public surface.

Purpose: ALLOW-05 ("the Treasurer derives the per-run `TokenBudget` from the remaining allowance and
works alongside `TokenBudget`, `ModelCallLimit`, `ToolCallLimit` and the Commissary") and the
mechanism of ALLOW-03's agent-loop halt (D-09..D-12).

Output: derivation, the Treasurer-aware `TokenBudget`, the composition proofs, registers.
</objective>
