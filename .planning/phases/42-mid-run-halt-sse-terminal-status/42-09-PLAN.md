<objective>
Extend the agent-loop cutoff to worker-dispatched agent-kind runs and close the agent-kind admission
gap at `POST /runs`, without letting the shared execution service cap engine nodes.

Task 1 installs `TokenBudget` in Treasurer-only mode on the shared service (G12) and teaches the
worker's `run_agent` to re-derive the budget at dispatch, pass it on the run's `RunScope`, and map an
`AllowanceHalted` stop (or a zero or unreadable figure at dispatch) to `Halted` with the reason
(D-12, G8, A8). Task 2 makes `POST /runs` admit agent-kind assistants with their model (D-09/D-10 at
the front door), maps the new refusal, proves the agent-kind halt end to end, records the D-08
window, and registers the change.

Purpose: D-13's coverage ("every principal-bearing agent path that Phase 41 admits") and ALLOW-03's
agent-loop halt on the worker path, with D-08's "no checkpoint" recorded rather than silent.

Output: Treasurer-only `TokenBudget` on the shared service, the worker agent-kind halt path,
`RunSubmissionError::ModelUnpriced`, an end-to-end proof, the WINDOWS.md D-08 row, registers.
</objective>
