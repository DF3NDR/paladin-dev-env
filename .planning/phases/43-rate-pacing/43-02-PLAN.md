<objective>
Expand the tracer so the provider's own retry delay reaches the gate: reshape
`LlmError::RateLimitExceeded` into a typed carrier (PACE-01's data contract), sweep every in-tree
consumer, make the decorator honour the delay as a minimum, and cap the gate at `max_wait_secs`
(D-06) by surfacing a typed error instead of tying up a worker.

Purpose: after this plan a caller (and a `RetryPolicy` above the decorator) can see and act on the
provider's own number, and a 1-hour `Retry-After` can no longer stall a worker silently. Header
parsing that fills the carrier from real responses lands in 43-03/43-04; here the carrier, its
semantics and the decorator's use of it are proven with scripted errors.

Output: `RateLimitHints` and friends (ports), the struct-shaped variant with constructors and
accessors, the swept workspace, the decorator's minimum-delay and D-06 rules, and the registered
surface.
</objective>
