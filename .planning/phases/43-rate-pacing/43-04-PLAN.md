<objective>
Close D-02 and PACE-01 for every remaining adapter: the compat engine (five presets), DeepSeek and
Gemini snapshot `Retry-After` through the shared parser's generic family and surface their first
429, and the shared conformance suite proves it for every fixture.

**Open Question 1 resolution (flagged):** D-02 names the OpenAI adapter, the compat engine and
the Anthropic adapter. DeepSeek and Gemini also run private loops that retry a 429 and both sit in
the conformance suite, so a once-only conformance case would fail for them. D-02's own rationale
("one layer sees every 429 the instant it happens ... no thrash hides below the decorator") covers
them, so this plan applies D-02 to DeepSeek and Gemini too -- a scope extension consistent with the
locked decision, rated reversible, surfaced to the operator in the phase return and ADR-0058.
Anthropic is not instantiated with `llm_conformance_suite!`; its once-only proof is the hand-written
test from 43-03. The OpenAI fixture runs with `max_retries: 0` (so the shared case is vacuous for
it); its once-only proof with `max_retries: 3` is `openai_429_is_surfaced_on_the_first_attempt`
(43-01) and `openai_429_carries_retry_after_and_ratelimit_dimensions` (43-03). The vision adapters'
`VisionError` loops are a different port and out of scope.

Output: three adapters wired, conformance case 10, the DeepSeek guard test flipped, the behaviour
registered.
</objective>
