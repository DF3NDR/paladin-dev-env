<objective>
Give the Cadence the distributed stampede lock (PACE-04's primitives): set-if-absent with expiry,
monotonically increasing fencing tokens with their source, delete-only-if-owner, in-process and
Redis implementations, fail-open in the resilient composite (D-13), and a fenced cache write at the
resource being protected (D-14). The engine's use of the lock is 43-11.

**Open Question 2 resolution (flagged conflict between locked decisions):** D-14 requires the
fencing token to reach the Redis `NodeCachePort` adapter's put path; D-11 says `NodeCachePort` is
unchanged (citing D-00d's best-effort contract). Both cannot hold literally -- a token nobody
checks is not fencing. This plan adds exactly one DEFAULTED trait method, `put_fenced`, delegating
to `put`: no existing method, signature, implementor or the D-29 best-effort contract changes, and
only `RedisNodeCache` overrides it. This is the one deliberate reading of D-00d/D-11 in this phase;
it is surfaced to the operator in the phase return and recorded in ADR-0058. If the operator rejects
it, the fallback is an inherent `RedisNodeCache::put_fenced` that the engine cannot reach through
`Arc<dyn NodeCachePort>` -- i.e. fencing would be lock-only, which the research shows is not fencing.

**Open Question 6 resolution (renewal vs TTL):** no lock renewal is built; `lock_ttl_secs`
defaults to 120 (43-01) and is documented as "must exceed the p99 node duration including retries";
fencing makes a late stale write harmless.

Output: lock methods and types on the port, three adapters, contract clauses, `put_fenced`, the
registered surface.
</objective>
