never streams live and never fires a webhook (WR-02, pinned by a tripwire test).

by `record_engine_failure` (so the graph `Err` path gains its missing `Failed` webhook too), the
inverted pinning test plus five new tests, rewritten docs, a CHANGELOG `Fixed` entry and the row 31
ledger closure.
