<objective>
Make the two context-window readers ration against the per-model window from the registry: a new
`effective_capabilities(port, model)` overlay, a `Commissary::for_model` constructor that uses it, and the
`HistoryTrimmer` resolving through it -- so `kimi-k3` is rationed against its real window instead of about
an eighth of it.

`WindowSource` variant (a public non-`#[non_exhaustive]` enum with `ALL: [WindowSource; 4]`).

Output: the overlay function, the constructor, the trimmer change, tests, baseline and changelog entries.
