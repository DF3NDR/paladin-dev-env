<objective>
Settle owner reading R-1 -- what CONTEXT D-01 and D-07 mean by "a run-metadata entry" -- and implement the reach the
owner chooses, so the visibility rule is decided by the owner rather than read narrowly by the planner.

Purpose: D-01 ("an auto-selected value is omitted ... made visible by a `warn!` and a run-metadata entry") and D-07
(the same rule for an unknown model). Plans 50-03, 50-05 and 50-14 stamp the keys on `LlmResponse.metadata`, but
`PaladinExecutionService` copies only `paladin.served_by` into `PaladinResult` (`paladin_execution_service.rs`
~1823), every `auto_temperature` omission happens on that builder/execution-service path, and streamed calls carry
no metadata at all -- so a caller of those paths sees only a `warn!`. Carrying the keys further is a registered
break on `PaladinResult` (constructible; the consumer muster builds it with a full literal), a one-way public-API
door, so the owner decides it here (`checkpoint:decision`), at the first point where every key exists (after plan
50-14) and the reach could be implemented. The plan sits in wave 14, after plan 50-15, because its option-b stream
change touches the adapter files 50-15 edits; plan 50-16 (wave 15) documents whichever reach is chosen.

Output: the ADR-0061 R-1 record; under option-b, `PaladinResult.run_metadata`, `StreamingResponse.metadata`, the
execution service and five stream paths carrying the keys, the migrated literals, the extended 9.2 row, baseline
and changelog entries.
</objective>
