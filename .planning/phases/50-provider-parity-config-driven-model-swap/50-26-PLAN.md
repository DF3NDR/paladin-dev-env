<objective>
Give `LlmError` a typed slot for the bill of a billed-but-failed call, migrate the remaining sites atomically, prove it
on one path (the compat engine's buffered call), and register the break in the same commit.

Every adapter runs the shared guard before mapping usage, so a call the vendor charged for and the guard classifies as
empty or refused returns no `TokenUsage`. The classic case is a reasoning model spending its whole `max_tokens`
budget on hidden reasoning (DeepSeek's fixture shows 63,000 tokens). The error-type change is the only way to carry
the figures out of `LlmPort::generate`.

Purpose: G-50-5a UAT missing item 3 and the owner's binding instruction that the Treasurer can settle billed-but-failed
calls (plans 50-27 to 50-30 finish it).
Split note (checker iteration 1 and the 2026-10-17 owner scope decision): with plan 50-25's additive groundwork in
place, this plan is the shape change plus its D-27 registration: 10 files. The registration must land in the same
commit: D-00b records a break in the same commit as the code, the lint table's own comment requires its lint, 9.2 row
and allowlist entry together, and CI's `semver` job fails on the tuple-to-struct change until the lint is allowed.
Reversibility (costly): `LlmError` is a published error type consumers match on. The change lands before the v0.11.0
tag with full registration, as D-09 and D-11 did for this phase's other error and response changes.

Output: `BilledUsage`, the two struct variants with accessors, `response_guards::bill`, the compat buffered path
billed, the remaining sites migrated, and the measured registration, in one commit.
</objective>
