<objective>
Count a hopped-past fallback bill toward the attempt's spend at the moment the chain hops past it. Today it waits
until the whole model call returns. That way a Battalion attempt cut off by its deadline or idle bound while a later
hop is still in flight is still charged for the earlier hop's billed refusal.

Purpose: review finding WR-03. Plan 50-29's promise is that an attempt's spend is counted however the attempt ends.
Plan 50-30 broke that promise for hop bills, which sit in the call's `HoppedBills` collector until `hopped.take()`
runs. Commit 0cdbc302 only documented the limit. The owner now wants the real fix. The review-fix analysis holds when
traced through the code. Recording the bill early but unpriced would be wrong: `IncurredSpend` makes the whole cost
unknown for good on any `None`. So the pricing decorator hands the collector a pricer before it delegates, the bill
is priced and counted at the hop, and a per-entry counted flag stops the service's later fold from counting it a
second time.

Output: two additive `HoppedBills` methods and the eager record in `paladin-ports`; `PricingLlmAdapter` installing
its pricer; the execution service taking collectors with `count_and_take` and no longer re-recording hop bills; and
paused-clock regression tests on the buffered path, the streamed path, the real Battalion engine and the pricing
decorator, each written and shown failing first. Also the docs, the CHANGELOG and MIGRATION text, and the ledger row.
</objective>

- A1-50-32: every bill `record_hopped_bill` records while an `ATTEMPT_SPEND` scope is active is counted at once,
  priced or not. With a pricing decorator anywhere in the stack, the pricer is installed before the chain runs, so a
  bill is counted unpriced only when nothing in the tree would price it later: an unpriced model, or no
  `PricingLlmAdapter` at all. That is exactly what the end-of-call fold would have recorded. An out-of-tree decorator
  that only calls `price_unpriced` after its inner call would see its late price reach the ledger row but not the
  attempt scope. The docs tell such a decorator to call `install_pricer` first. (Owner question Q1, open.)
