<objective>
Give the shared compat engine (Kimi, Qwen, Grok, Ollama, OpenAI-compatible) a compile-enforced reasoning wire
declaration and exact per-wire mapping, with refusal before HTTP for anything the wire or the model cannot honour, and
let the two self-hosted presets take an operator-declared wire.

Purpose: REASON-02 (every preset states a non-`bool` `ReasoningWire`; generic OpenAI-compatible defaults to
`Unsupported`), REASON-03 (no preset emits a field its vendor ignores), REASON-04 (levels from the masked per-model
profile). D-00f, D-00i, D-04, D-10. Kimi is the tracer: it is the provider whose default (`max`) motivated the phase.

Output: the reshaped `CompatRequestParameters`, four new skip-if-`None` request fields, per-wire mapping and masking,
`with_reasoning_wire` on two configs, the registered break, CHANGELOG entries and a refreshed baseline.
</objective>
