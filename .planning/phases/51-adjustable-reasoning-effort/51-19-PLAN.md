<objective>
Close G-2 item (3) and the chain half of item (2): `FallbackLlmAdapter` overrides `LlmPort::check_reasoning` (plan
51-17) by asking every hop its own verdict against its own step model, its pre-flight becomes that call, and its
`model_profile` stops silently dropping a hop that reports no profile from the effort it advertises.

Purpose: D-08 pre-flights every hop "before the first HTTP call" and D-09 gives a chain a build-time check. Today the
builder judges a chain against an intersection that skips profile-less hops (accepts what the pre-flight refuses) and
over-refuses touching level sets, and the pre-flight ignores each hop's provider defaults. One per-hop verdict removes
all three.

Output: the override, the delegating pre-flight, the fail-closed chain-profile effort, rustdoc, and chain tests in
`fallback.rs` and `paladin_builder.rs`, red-first.

<assumption_delta_decision>
Detector fired (`pluralization`: "per-hop models in fallback chains"; `chosen`). Identity question: what noun does an
effort verdict belong to once one port can stand for several hops?

- **Primary noun: the hop** (a port plus its step model plus that port's own provider defaults). `check_reasoning`
  per hop is the authority for every effort verdict on a chain -- build, pre-flight and send.
- **Decision: promote.** The per-hop verdict replaces the intersected chain profile as the effort authority; the
  intersection is demoted to a detail of `model_profile` (still used for sampling, where its leniency is intended, and
  failing closed for effort when any hop reports no profile). Not add-alongside: the builder stops reading the chain
  profile's effort at all (plan 51-17 already routes it through `check_reasoning`).
- Suggested contract test (included): `a_chain_check_reasoning_is_the_and_of_every_hops_check` -- for a small grid of
  hop level sets and fallbacks, the chain verdict equals the conjunction of the hop verdicts, and its refusal names the
  lowest refusing index.
</assumption_delta_decision>
