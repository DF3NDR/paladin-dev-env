The scouting settled the shape of the roadmap's decision point. There is no threads table; a thread
is only `waypoints.thread_id` plus `runs.thread_id`. `Waypoint` (paladin-ai-core), `ThreadSummary`
and `WaypointPort` (paladin-ports) and `JobRecord` (paladin-web) are all-public-field types with no
`#[non_exhaustive]`, so recording a tenant on the thread is a public break under the patch-level
semver gate and forces v0.12.0. Deriving the tenant from the thread's latest run (what
`ensure_thread_visible` already does for submit and fork) is additive and keeps v0.11.1; its costs
are a per-thread run lookup on `GET /v1/threads` and the run-less-thread case, which area 2 covers.

published_run_id_operations` is the route-matrix precedent for criterion 1. For TENANT-07, keep the
owner inside `JobStore`'s private state rather than on the public `JobRecord`. For TENANT-08 the
assertion can sit in `build_run_api` or as an additive `RunWorkerPool` method.
